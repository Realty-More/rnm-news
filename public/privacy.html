Privacy Policy

Effective 27 October 2026 · Applies to the Realtynmore app (Android, iPhone and web).

In short: readers don't need an account. There are no ads, we don't sell or rent data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. Saved stories and reading history stay on your phone. Team members who log in can delete their account at any time.

1. Who we are

The Realtynmore app is published by Realty & More (RealtyNMore), the real estate news publisher behind realtynmore.com and the Realty & More magazine. Contact: desk@realtynmore.com.

2. Readers (no account)

Kept only on your phone

  • Saved stories, reading history ("Continue reading"), offline copies of stories, and settings such as topics, cities, language, theme and text size.
  • You can clear reading history in Settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes all of this.

Anonymous usage counts (you can turn this off)

  • Counts of which stories and screens are opened, search words typed in the app, and how often stories are shared, added up per day. They power "Trending" and help us improve coverage.
  • These counts contain no name, phone number, device ID, advertising ID, location, IP address or cookie. Turn them off in Settings → Privacy → Share anonymous usage counts.

Notifications (optional)

  • If you allow news alerts, we keep the delivery address your phone or browser gives us and the topics you chose, only to send those alerts. It expires after 180 days of no renewal and is deleted when you turn alerts off.

Weekly newsletter (optional)

  • If you subscribe, we keep your email address, the name you give (optional), and when you signed up, confirmed and received each issue. We use it only to send the Realty & More weekly newsletter.
  • You confirm your subscription from a link in the first email. Every newsletter has a one-click unsubscribe link. Emails are sent through Brevo, our email delivery provider.

Ask R&M (AI assistant)

  • Your question is sent to our server and answered only from Realty & More stories, using Cloudflare Workers AI. We don't store questions with anything that identifies you. An answer may be cached for a few hours so the same question is answered faster.
  • To enforce the daily question limit we keep a count per day under a one-way code made from your network address and the date. The code changes every day and can't be turned back into the address.

3. Team members (Studio login)

Realty & More staff can log in to the Studio inside the app. For them we keep:

  • Account: name, mobile number, password (stored only as a salted, one-way hash, never in readable form), role, and login times. Login sessions last up to 180 days and end on log out.
  • Work records: attendance check-in and check-out times with the mode you choose (office, remote or field; no GPS location), daily work reports, assigned tasks, and leave requests.
  • This is visible only to the owner and staff with full access, and is used to manage work and prepare salaries.
  • Wrong password attempts are counted for an hour to stop guessing.

4. Content from other services

  • Stories and photos load from realtynmore.com. Magazine issues load from realtynmore.com through our server.
  • Videos play through YouTube (Google), and market prices show through TradingView. When you open these, those services receive your request under their own privacy policies.

5. Where data is kept and how it's protected

  • The app's files are hosted on GitHub Pages. Our server and database run on Cloudflare.
  • All connections use HTTPS encryption. Studio data needs a login, and owner-only sections are checked on the server.
  • We don't sell, rent or share personal data with advertisers or data brokers.

7. Deleting your account and data

  • Team members: in the app, open the Studio and tap Delete account, or use the Delete account page from any browser. This deletes your name, mobile number, password and login sessions at once. Attendance, leave, task and report entries are company work records and are kept by Realty & More.
  • Newsletter: tap Unsubscribe in any newsletter email. To delete your email from our list completely, write to desk@realtynmore.com.
  • Readers: there is no account. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it, and turn off notifications to remove the alert address.
  • You can also email desk@realtynmore.com to ask what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it.

8. Children

The app is a general real estate news service and isn't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date above.
